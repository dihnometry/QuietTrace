The before board is the baseline (all lines on L1). The after board is the lowest-cost state that the optimized circuit actually produces — the min-cost entry of the distribution, feasible ones preferred, never a post-selected state the circuit does not emit. Conflicts come from the real intersection of the routed traces.

Solution evolution · during optimization (argmax state per iteration)

Each mini-PCB is the most probable state at that iteration of the local optimization (not the final circuit's output). It is not monotonic: the state can move back and forth while β and γ are still being tuned.

QPU run (backend selected in Qollab)

Optimization runs locally; one QAOA circuit is sent to the selected backend (Qollab enforces one job per run on IonQ). Simulators run in your browser or on IonQ's cloud and are free; only the IonQ quantum computers spend credits. The local preview has no backend, so this section stays empty.
